Crusoe: new prices posted for 1 Dec

From the 6 Nov page

Crusoe's pricing page as of 6 Nov has two columns, "Current on-demand" and "New on-demand starting Dec 1, 2026". The new column lists the B300 at $13.80 and the B200 at $9.65; the tracking sites currently record Crusoe's B300 at $8.96.

The sites have read Crusoe's higher prices once before. From 16 to 21 Oct they recorded its H100 at $4.50 in place of $3.90 and its H200 at $5.40 in place of $4.29, then went back. Because the engine replays that episode, our 7-day high forecasts for the gpurentalprices H200 and B300 lists already sit at the levels a repeat would produce ($5.25 and $10.59).

A pattern worth keeping

When a provider that rarely reprices changed a price by more than 2% on one tracking site, the other site showed the same price within a day in all 32 past cases in the pack. Lambda is the exception so far: FastGPU had not matched a 3 Nov change by 6 Nov, which suggests it reads Lambda's prices from a different place.

What stays open

One exposed series: gpurentalprices B200

Its median jumped from $6.89 to $7.89 on 6 Nov, set by Lambda's new prices. Both the engine and the agents hold it flat at about $7.89. If the site goes back to reading Lambda's current prices before 13 Nov, the median drops by a dollar and the forecast misses at all three horizons.

The only precedent is Crusoe's reversal in October. A rough guess is a 10 to 15% chance, which is too thin to justify changing the forecast by hand. Drift and the last value carry the same exposure.

Rules we kept

  • Nothing after 6 Nov. Every price-related request was pinned to the end of 6 Nov, by snapshot date or by publication date.
  • Search without snapshots can leak. Exa returns today's copy of a page unless a snapshot date is set. Two search results carried later text and were discarded.
  • Excluded sources stayed excluded. Nothing was taken from the providers and marketplaces the data pack rules out.
  • Every lead was checked against the pack before it was treated as a fact.
