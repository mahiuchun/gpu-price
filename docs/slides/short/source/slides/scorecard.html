FINDINGS.md · Summary and §3, §5, §9, §10

[7:45–9:00] Two more honest points to say here. Ranges held the truth 80% of the time overall, but only 66% when the price actually moved. And the result does not hang on one model: four other API models in the same crew cut error by 15% to 22%. The honest scorecard. Two loops held up: the coach, and RL training a model to forecast directly. Three did not: tuning the engine with an AI scientist, RL to propose engine settings, and letting agents read each other. Two gave small or unproven gains: three kinds of memory, and an AI analyst that found real timing patterns that did not translate into better forecasts.
