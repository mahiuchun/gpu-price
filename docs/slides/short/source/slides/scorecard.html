FINDINGS.md · Summary and §3, §5, §9, §10

[7:56–9:00] Now the honest part. We tried seven ways to make the system improve itself. Two held up on fresh days: the coach, and reinforcement learning that teaches a small open model to forecast. Three did not. An AI scientist tuning the engine actually made it slightly worse on unseen weeks. And two gave small gains we can’t call established. Two more caveats. Our ranges held the truth eighty percent of the time overall, but only sixty-six percent of the time when the price actually moved, and that’s the number a lender cares about. On the other hand, the result doesn’t hang on one model: four other API models in the same crew cut error by fifteen to twenty-two percent.
