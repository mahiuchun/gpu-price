[7:08–7:56] One test window is thin, so we checked three: the eight weeks before our test, the three test weeks themselves, and four years of AWS spot prices, almost thirty-six thousand forecasts. The engine was a steady eight percent ahead of drift in all three. The five agents were fifteen to nineteen percent ahead in all three. And notice the orange bars: an off-the-shelf machine learning model swung from worse than drift to clearly ahead, depending on the period, which is exactly why we didn’t rely on it.
