[2:45–3:30] This is the insight the engine is built on. For neocloud list prices, two thirds of the day-to-day movement in the published median is the sample changing — offers appearing or disappearing — not providers repricing. A quarter comes from a couple of offers that reprice almost daily. Hyperscaler spot is the opposite: almost no list churn, and movement comes from repricing.
