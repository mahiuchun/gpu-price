[3:16–3:56] Why does looking inside the list help so much? Because of this. For neocloud list prices, sixty-six percent of the day-to-day movement in the published median comes from offers joining or leaving the list. Another twenty-five percent comes from one or two offers that reprice almost daily. Only nine percent is everyone else actually changing their price. So if you only watch the median, you’re mostly watching the sample change.
