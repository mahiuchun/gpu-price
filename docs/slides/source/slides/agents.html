The forecast we hand in comes from here. Five agents built on the same model, each with a different job, forecast each price directly from the facts. The coach sees the crowd’s scores and worst misses on practice days and proposes one change at a time: rewrite an agent, add one, drop one, or stretch the ranges. Of ten proposals, two passed the gate. The two kept changes were wider ranges at 3 and 7 days, which carry nearly all of the gain, and a rewritten base-rate keeper.
