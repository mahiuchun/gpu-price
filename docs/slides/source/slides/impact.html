README.md · Overview

Three kinds of impact. For anyone who prices GPUs: a forecast with ranges for lease pricing, GPU-backed lending and capacity planning, plus practical rules for reading published prices. For recursive self-improvement: a working pattern in which an AI proposes, a verifier it cannot change decides, and a final exam on unseen data checks the result — a loop that can be pointed at other agent crews and other forecasting tasks. For honest evaluation: every idea, including the failures, is logged and can be replayed, and the forecast rebuilds in about 15 seconds with no AI calls.
