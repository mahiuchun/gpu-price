Replay what each offer did, then take the middle again

1 · Start from today’s list

It knows which offer sits in the middle and how far its neighbours let the middle move.

2 · Replay past days

For each past pair of days: who repriced, who left, who rejoined.

3 · Read off the range

The spread of replayed medians gives q10, q50 and q90.

Illustrative numbers · src/gpuval/engine.py

Drift, the reference forecast, replays how the median itself moved. The engine looks underneath: for a horizon of h days it takes every past pair of days, applies to today’s list what each individual offer did between them — repriced, stayed, dropped off, rejoined — and recomputes the median. It is classic historical simulation; nothing is trained. The animation uses illustrative numbers.
