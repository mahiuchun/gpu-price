FOR SOMEONE PRICING A LEASE OR A LOAN

How to read a published GPU price

Read the median with its list

A move is far more likely an offer entering or leaving than a change in what providers charge.

Check the currency of the middle offer

A euro price converted each morning moves Tuesday to Saturday whatever the GPU market does.

Ask how often a range held when the price moved

80% overall can be 66% on the days that matter.

Read AWS spot as a share of list price

The 2026 rise is that share climbing from about 0.3 to 0.5 with list unchanged.

FINDINGS.md · What someone pricing a lease or a loan could use

Practical takeaways beyond the leaderboard. A published median is a property of the sample as much as of the market. Check which offer is in the middle and what currency it is priced in. Judge a forecaster by how often its range held when the price actually moved. And for AWS spot, think in terms of its share of list price: a list cut would pull spot down within days, and nothing in spot history warns of one.
