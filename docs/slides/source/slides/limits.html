LIMITS

What these results do not show

Later forecasters were not blind

The agents, coach, ML model and RL forecaster were added after the blind weeks had been looked at.

Samples, not every day

The agents were scored on 19 days of our prices and 15 days of AWS.

One coach run, noisy RL

The coach ran once. One RL seed change moved a result from +10.1% to +6.5%.

The model may know AWS history

Its knowledge runs to April 2026; only four AWS test days are later. It recalled none of them.

The 37 were picked with hindsight

On the pack’s 476 other prices the engine’s edge is about half: +4.6%.

Posted, not transacted

These are list prices, not what anyone actually paid.

FINDINGS.md · Limits · PROTOCOL.md · Deviations

We want the limits to be as visible as the results. The blind test covered the engine and the AI scientist; everything after was tested on weeks we had already looked at, though on days the models had not seen. Agent tests use samples. RL is noisy across seeds. The model’s training may include older AWS history. The 37 prices were chosen by the starter kit with hindsight, and on other prices the engine’s edge is about half. And all of this is posted prices.
