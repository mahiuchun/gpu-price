The inference pipeline. The crowd that came out of training is frozen: the five agents’ instructions, including the one the coach rewrote, and the range stretch. For each of the 37 prices on 6 November, the facts — the last 21 days of the published median and today’s list of offers, plus reference forecasts for the reference weigher — go to the five agents. Each returns a low, middle and high for 1, 3 and 7 days. These are averaged, then the ranges are stretched by 1.25 at 3 days and 1.65 at 7 days. The result is the 111-cell forecast file we hand in. There is no feedback and nothing changes during inference. On fresh days this crowd made 20% less error than drift, with a likely range of 10% to 28%.
